MUSD Pay Links

Mezo Buildathon, Access and Distribution track

A merchant asks for MUSD with a standard EIP-681 link, a QR code or a pay page sent in a chat. Before the payer's wallet or agent builds the transfer, the guard checks that the token is the real MUSD for that chain, that the recipient address is valid, and that the amount is within the payer's limit. A payer who is short of MUSD sees how much BTC would mint it in the MUSD protocol. The merchant finds the payment on chain by its invoice reference.

  1. Create an invoice in panel 1. Share the QR code or the pay page.
  2. Check the link as the payer in panel 2. Try the bad links to see the guard refuse them.
  3. Short of MUSD? Panel 4 reads the live MUSD protocol and quotes the BTC to lock.
  4. Watch for the payment in panel 1, or record a transaction hash in panel 3. An invoice is paid at 5 confirmations.

Reading the contracts...

1. Merchant, request MUSD

Saves an invoice with a unique reference, stamps it with the current block, and gives you the link, the QR code and a pay page. The wallet of the recipient signs the invoice (a free message, not a transaction), so nobody can create invoices for your address. The link is run through the payer guard before it is shown.

Invoices for this address

2. Payer, check before paying

Paste any link. The checks run in your browser with the same library the API uses.

Try a bad link

3. Merchant, confirm the payment on chain

Reads the transaction receipt and counts MUSD Transfer events from the real MUSD contract to the address you enter. With a reference, the payment must carry it. With an invoice block, a payment mined earlier is refused. A payment is final at 5 confirmations. With a reference, "Record invoice as paid" saves the payment in that invoice, checked against the stored invoice. Prefilled with the project's own 5 MUSD payment of invoice INV-65ZVUMXW on Mezo Testnet, made on 26 October 2026. Creating an invoice in panel 1 fills this form with that invoice.

4. Payer, get MUSD with BTC (live MUSD protocol)

MUSD is minted against BTC. This quote reads the live rules from the MUSD protocol contracts on Mezo (BorrowerOperations, TroveManager, PriceFeed and InterestRateManager). It shows the BTC to lock, the fee, the protocol minimum and the liquidation price. Nothing is signed here. The borrow itself happens in the Mezo app.
